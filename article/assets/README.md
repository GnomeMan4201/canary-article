# Canary article cover

`canary-cover.png` was created with the built-in image generation tool and copied into this project. It is an editorial illustration, not a visualization of experiment data.

Alt text: A yellow canary perched on a dark software terminal beside a separate checklist, drawn in ink on warm cream paper.

Final prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide editorial cover illustration for a developer article titled The Art of the Canary in the Age of AI. Primary request: a thoughtful, beautifully composed illustration of a small golden yellow canary perched on the edge of a minimal software terminal, with a separate sheet of precise test-check boxes beside it, communicating that a visible signal and checked behavior are distinct observations. Match the companion report palette of warm cream paper, dark charcoal ink, muted olive details and canary yellow. Style: sophisticated editorial ink drawing with subtle screen-printed texture, generous negative space, crisp shapes and a natural expressive bird. Wide landscape composition suitable for an article cover; keep the important subjects within the central safe area. The terminal contains a few abstract code-like lines but no readable wording. No title text, no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no invented data or results, no green/red success or failure badges, no neon or glossy 3D. Opaque warm paper background.

## Psychedelic alternate

`canary-cover-psychedelic.png` was generated with the built-in tool using the original cover as an edit reference. The article currently uses this version; the original is preserved.

Alt text: A yellow canary, software terminal, and separate checklist surrounded by psychedelic pop-art birds and hand-drawn shapes.

Final prompt:

> Create a radically transformed alternate cover based on this reference image. Preserve the core subjects and idea: a canary, a software terminal, and a separate checklist, for a developer article about AI instruction canaries. Reimagine it as exuberant psychedelic pop art: Andy Warhol-inspired silkscreen repetition, aggressively saturated acid yellow, hot pink, electric cyan, orange and purple, thick black ink, halftone grain, deliberately imperfect print registration. A large iconic canary dominates, with echoes of the canary in contrasting color blocks. Around and between the large shapes, fill space with original freehand cartoon doodles: looping cables, curious eyes, tiny birds, wobbly code brackets, warped keyboards, suns and surreal desert-festival shapes, playful densely interlocking marks. Make it feel like an underground art poster dreamed up at a wild desert festival after midnight. Chaotic and ecstatic but with an unmistakable central canary silhouette that reads at thumbnail size. Flat graphic artwork rather than a realistic desk scene. No readable text, no words, no logos, no watermark. No scientific data or claims. Wide landscape article-cover format. This is an original playful doodle and pop-art treatment, not a copy of any living artist's distinctive style.
